I've been building e-commerce systems since 2011 and I'm the founder of
[Codemanufacture](https://codemanufacture.com/), where we build, tune and look
after Magento stores.

I've been a [nixpkgs](https://github.com/NixOS/nixpkgs) maintainer since 2024
and I'm part of its PHP team, keeping PHP and tools in shape, with over 200 merged changes.
I also contribute to open source across PHP, Emacs and Nix, including docker-magento and Magento itself.

I care about software that is simple, reproducible and built to last.
